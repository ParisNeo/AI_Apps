How CryptaVault Works

CryptaVault is a secure, front-end only application for storing your sensitive data.

Key Features & Security:

  • Local Storage: All your data (passwords, notes, files) is stored exclusively on your device's browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is ever sent to any server.
  • Master Password: You secure your vault with a Master Password. This password is used to derive an encryption key. It is never stored. If you forget it, your data is permanently inaccessible. There is no recovery option.
  • Strong Encryption:
    • Your Master Password is processed with PBKDF2 (a key derivation function) using a unique salt to create a strong encryption key.
    • All your data items are individually encrypted using AES-GCM, a robust and authenticated encryption algorithm. This means your data is both confidential and protected against tampering.
  • Data Types: You can store passwords (with username, website, notes), general notes, and files (images, PDFs, documents), which are stored base64 encoded and then encrypted.
  • Export/Import: You can export all your encrypted data as a JSON file. This file contains your encrypted items and the salt needed for your Master Password. You can import this file back into CryptaVault (on the same or a different device/browser). The import will replace any existing data.
  • Security Considerations:
    • The security of CryptaVault relies on the strength of your Master Password and the security of your device and browser.
    • Ensure your device is free from malware. Malicious software (like keyloggers) or compromised browser extensions could potentially intercept your Master Password as you type it.
    • Your derived encryption key exists in your browser's memory only while the vault is unlocked. Closing the tab or browser clears it.

Usage:

  1. First Use: Create a strong Master Password. Remember it!
  2. Unlocking: Enter your Master Password to access your vault.
  3. Adding Items: Click "Add New Item", choose the type, fill in the details, and save.
  4. Viewing/Editing: Click on an item in the list to view its details. Editing is available from the view.
  5. Deleting: Delete items from their view/edit screen.
  6. Export: Click "Export Data" to save an encrypted backup of your vault. Keep this file safe.
  7. Import: Click "Import Data" and select your previously exported JSON file. Warning: Importing will overwrite any current data in the vault.
  8. Logout: Click "Logout" to lock your vault. It also locks automatically when you close the browser/tab.
