    How to Play

    Stop invaders!

    • Profiles: Create/select to save progress.
    • Towers: Select, then tap grid to place. Costs gold.
    • Gold: Earn from invaders & waves.
    • Lives: Start 20. 0 = Game Over.
    • Waves: Tap "Start Wave".
    • Unlocks: Turret(Start), Cannon(Lvl 4), Freezer(Lvl 7).
    • Level Reset: Towers sold (full refund).
    • Progress: Saved per profile.
